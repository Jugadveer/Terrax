How it works

The whole path, with nothing hidden behind a status light. If a step produces a number, this page says where the number comes from.

The seller puts the property on the record

Five steps: photographs, specification, documents, price, review. Each step is validated on the server, so a listing cannot skip ahead with half its fields empty. Drafts are saved as you go and can be picked up later from the same place.

Photographs1 to 12
Required fields6

Draft expiryNone

Documents are checked as they arrive

The file type is read from the leading bytes rather than the extension. Scans below roughly 700 by 500 pixels are flagged as unreadable. Expiry dates are parsed where the document has one. A SHA-256 checksum catches the same file being reused on a second listing.

FormatPDF, JPEG, PNG
Maximum size15 MB
Duplicate detectionSHA-256
OutcomeShown to buyers

An independent valuation is calculated

Comparable listings are chosen by property type, then locality, city and state in turn. Each is scored on size, location, configuration, age and how recently it was listed. Comparables from a different city are rebased onto local rates before their price per square foot is used. The weighted result is then adjusted for age, furnishing, amenities, aspect, floor, title and carpet ratio.

A risk score is produced from what can be checked

Seven weighted factors totalling one hundred: missing documents, failed document checks, the seller's identity status, how far the asking price sits above the valuation, how complete the listing record is, the type of title, and how long it has been on the market. Every factor that contributed is shown with its points and the reason it fired.

Seller identity20 points

Buyers offer, or buy a fraction

An offer opens a thread that both sides keep. Either side can counter, which restarts the seven day window. Accepting one closes the rest, and the buyers are told why. Where the seller has enabled fractional ownership, shares can be bought from the unsold pool instead, and sold back to it at the current share price.

Offer window7 days
Counter offersUnlimited
Smallest position1 share
Platform fee0.5% on purchase

What this build is, and is not

Terrax is a working demonstration, not a licensed marketplace. Listings and transactions are seeded data. Identity documents are reviewed by hand rather than by a verification provider, and no money moves.
