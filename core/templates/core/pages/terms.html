Terms

Short, because this is a demonstration rather than a marketplace.

What Basix is

A demonstration of a property marketplace, built as a student project. It is not a registered broker, not a licensed exchange, and not a financial services provider. No money moves through it and no property changes hands on it.

The listings

The listings loaded by default are seeded demonstration data. The addresses, prices and documents behind them are fabricated. Anything you list yourself is your own responsibility, and you should not upload genuine title documents to this build.

Valuations and risk scores

Both are calculated from the listings in this system alone. They are illustrations of a method, not a professional valuation or a survey, and they carry no warranty of accuracy. Do not make a financial decision on the basis of a number shown here.

Offers, shares and the ledger

Offers, share purchases and ledger entries are recorded in the database and settle nothing. An accepted offer creates a record; it does not create a contract, and no legal obligation follows from it.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself and do not reuse one from elsewhere. You are responsible for what is submitted from your account. Accounts that upload unlawful material will be removed.

Availability

The service is provided as it is, with no guarantee of uptime, and may be taken down or reset without notice.
