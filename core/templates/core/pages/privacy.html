Privacy

What this build stores, where it stores it, and what it does not store.

What is collected

An account holds a username, an email address, an optional mobile number, and whatever you choose to put on your profile. Listings hold the property details and files you upload. Offers hold the amounts and messages you send. Watchlists and saved searches hold what you chose to save.

Identity documents

When you submit an identity document, the file is stored and only the last four characters of the document number are kept alongside it. A full PAN or Aadhaar number is never written to the database, so a database dump cannot leak one.

Wallet addresses

A wallet address, if you connect one, is stored as a plain string. Terrax never asks for and never holds a private key or a seed phrase. No page on this site will ever ask you for either.

What is not collected

No analytics scripts, no advertising trackers, no third party fonts, and no third party stylesheets. The typeface and the icon set are served from this domain. The only outbound request the site makes is to a language model provider, and only when one has been configured by the operator.

Cookies

Two: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a CSRF token that protects forms from being submitted by another site. Both are strictly necessary, so there is no consent banner to dismiss. Your theme choice is kept in local storage in your own browser.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a listing removes it and its files. There is no account deletion flow in this build; to have an account removed, use the contact form.

This is a student project

Terrax is a demonstration, not a licensed service. Do not upload a real identity document, and do not reuse a password from another site.
